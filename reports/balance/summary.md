# Crafting balance

Generated by `lune run tools/balance.luau` (model: `tests/lib/balance.luau`, targets: `Constants.BALANCE_TARGETS`).

**Value** = seconds an item saves its owner per race, on average over every partner build: its stats (pace fitted to KineSim races) + its E ability (fired by a sensible player in a KineSim race; a harmful one = hits per race × the seconds one hit costs the rival) + protection (shield / lift uptime × the hits a racer takes). **Matches**: Monte Carlo fields of 6 players with farmed inventories (spawn odds `RARITY_SPAWN_DIST`), each crafting the core items worth most; race time = pace − own gains + hits taken + driver noise (σ 2 s).
